# Extended Data Fig. X. Exploratory pathway-level summaries of target-versus-control perturbation responses.

For each context and perturbation target, genes were ranked by the signed mean expression difference between perturbed cells and matched control cells, and pre-ranked fgsea (R Bioconductor `fgsea`, Korotkevich et al., Nat Methods 2021; adaptive multilevel permutation test, eps = 1e-10, minSize = 10, maxSize = 500, set.seed(42)) was applied to MSigDB Hallmark gene sets. Displayed pathways were selected from a pre-specified perturbation-response panel covering cell death/stress, proliferation, proteostasis, metabolic state, state-remodeling and inflammatory/interferon axes. Heatmap colors show normalized enrichment scores (NES), and asterisks indicate pathways passing FDR < 0.10. Targets were selected by benchmark-relevant anchor rules and NES-variance ranking, not by the appearance of pathway enrichment results. These analyses provide a bounded biological landing layer and were not used to define the benchmark truth object, endpoint hierarchy or model-adjudication criteria.
